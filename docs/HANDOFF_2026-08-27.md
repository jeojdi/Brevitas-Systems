### Verified, with an important framing correction

**Wire-level resend tax is REAL and larger than the 20–40% estimate — but mostly NOT recoverable.**
Across 5,019 sessions / 81,635 deduped billed requests / 11.18B wire prompt tokens (provider receipts):
novel tokens are 346,982,072 of 11,181,738,816 = **96.90% resend, 32.2× amplification**.

Do **not** quote 78.55% as savings. Attention must re-read the prefix; the 0.10× cache-read rate is the
price of that. The addressable slice is the **33.13% that is cache writes** ($1,608.09 of $4,854.47),
and 46.7% of write tokens sit in a ≤10s fan-out window with a free documented fix — which we already
ship as `BatchGroupGate` (`token_efficiency_model/lossless/batch_group.py`, wired in
`brevitas/proxy.py:1425-1494` and `:1583-1669`).

Other verified items: agent tool-loop headroom is ~41% blended cost/task and **quality went UP**
(0.78→0.81) — the "43% at 96.7%" pair does not exist. Multimodal formulas verified (1024×1024 at
`detail:high` = 1,229 billable tokens); **video bills per second ($0.10–$0.70/s), invisible to every
token-schema tool**. Eval/CI compute waste is **still unverified and the denominator does not exist** —
nobody splits compute into pretrain/post-train/serve/eval and no eval framework reports GPU-hours.

## 7. The research method that worked (reuse this)

Both runs used the same shape and it is worth keeping:

1. **Fan out scouts** over narrow segments (12 worked well; 6 was too coarse).
2. **Adversarial kill pass** — one agent per candidate, instructed "your job is to KILL this, default
   to killing it," required to produce a kill argument even if it concludes the idea survives.
3. **Synthesis** agent that must surface contradictions between agents rather than smoothing them.

**Enforce the exclusion list in every scout prompt** or they re-discover dead ideas. Currently barred:
custom kernels of any kind, 2–3-bit serving, activation sparsity, DVFS/energy licensing, spot arbitrage,
routing-as-product, prompt compression, PD-disaggregation, KV transfer, cache-aware routing, multi-LoRA,
RL-rollout serving, KV tiering, sparse-attention features, semantic prefix matching, cross-engine KV
standard, expert offload, raw expert pruning, adapter composition, cold-start tooling, eviction-policy
tuning, live speculator adaptation, benchmarking-as-a-product.

**Force a `why_uncrowded` field** on every candidate, and explicitly allow "it is empty because it does
not work and people failed quietly" as a successful answer. That field caught more than anything else.

### Two methodological findings worth keeping

**An active GitHub issue tracker is the work being done, not a gap.** Six of ten scouts built their case
on issue counts; every one was refuted. Example: "263 open ROCm accuracy issues" was open **plus closed**
— only 75 open, meaning 188 divergences were already fixed. Issue volume proxies *staffing*, not vacancy.

**"You'd need an unusual skill pair" is not a moat.** Failed in 8 of 10 reviews. Seven papers, an open
repo, and zero startups is a well-explored area where nobody found a buyer — not an unclaimed seat.

## 8. Environment gotchas

**Web search budget — FIXED TODAY, needs a restart.** All 22 agents in the second run got **zero**
searches: the session-wide cap of 200 was already spent by the first run, before any of them made a
call. The cap silently returns a normal-looking result containing refusal text rather than erroring,
so agents read "no results" and reported "nobody ships this" when they simply could not look.
This has now degraded three research sessions.

`CLAUDE_CODE_MAX_WEB_SEARCHES_PER_SESSION=1000` was added to `~/.claude/settings.json` env
(backup at `settings.json.bak`). **It only takes effect after a Claude Code restart** — env is read at
session start, and the counter resets then too. The budget is shared across the whole session including
every subagent, so it is a ceiling, not a per-agent allocation; keep writing per-agent search discipline
into prompts.

**Consequence for reading today's reports:** paper-, repo- and source-level findings are solid (direct
fetches, arXiv Atom API, authenticated `gh`). **Company-level discovery is not.** Every "this seat is
open" verdict is a floor, not a census. The kills are more trustworthy than the discoveries, because
killing required finding an incumbent *positively*.

unprofiled; no usable data on Salad/io.net/Render/Nosana/Vast.ai/Hyperbolic (403s and JS-only SPAs),
